The word sits
inside the hill.

Not on top of it. Six independent depth planes, each drawn by hand at runtime, and the typography threaded between them.

↓

01 / occlusion

Depth is what hides things.

Most parallax sites move layers at different speeds. That is motion, not depth. Depth is when something in front actually takes something behind away from you.

Here the near ridge climbs across the wordmark as you scroll and the grass closes over the lower third of the letters. The type is genuinely behind them in the compositing order, not covered by an overlay.

02 / construction

Every ridge is a sine sum.

Three out-of-phase bands, each with its own frequency and roughness, sampled into a polygon and filled. Change the seed and you get a different mountain — no image assets, no SVG files, nothing to download.

03 / restraint

One animation frame, six canvases.

Everything reads from a single scroll value in one requestAnimationFrame pass, and each canvas is only redrawn when its offset actually changed. Nothing is animated on a timer, so an idle page costs nothing.

On a phone the far planes drop to half resolution and the blur passes are skipped entirely — the effect survives, the cost does not.

That is the whole idea.

A single mechanic, executed precisely, rather than twelve effects fighting each other.
